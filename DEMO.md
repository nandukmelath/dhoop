# Dhoop in three minutes

The order follows the five steps the organisers ask every demo to show: initiate, understand, execute, confirm, follow up. Say each line as written, or the same thing in your own language.

## Before the judges arrive

- Open https://dhoop.onrender.com on the laptop, in Chrome, and press **Start talking** about half a minute early. If Dhoop says "Sorry, I missed that", it has just recovered its hearing: say the next line again.
- In a loud hall, switch on **Loud room** and hold the space bar while speaking.
- Keep the phone open on the same address as a second screen for the passbook.

## The demo

| Step | Say | What the judges see |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Initiate | "My electricity bill is 3,200 rupees a month, and I live in Bengaluru." | The bill is read and the sunshine for Bengaluru is fetched from NASA. |
| 2. Understand | "Show me the Vidhana Soudha in Bengaluru." (or "Find my roof" with location on) | The satellite view dives to the building, traces its roof and lays panels on it. |
| 3. Execute | "What should I install?" | A system sized to the bill: subsidy, saving a month, payback. |
| 3. Execute | "Add 300 square metres on the right." then "Undo that." | The roof on the picture changes by voice, and changes back. |
| 4. Confirm | "Can I get the subsidy?" then answer its four questions | It says plainly what must be put right before applying. |
| 4. Confirm | "Who installs solar here?" then "Get me quotes" and say yes | Real registered vendors with phone numbers; the survey goes out only after a clear yes. |
| 5. Follow up | "Explain it to my mother in Kannada." | Dhoop comes back speaking Kannada and sums up in two sentences. |
| 5. Follow up | "Play my solar story." | A half-minute film of this household's own plan, in the same language. |

End on the passbook: scan the code on the screen with the phone. The plan, its voice note and the visit's calendar entry are what the household takes home.

## If something goes wrong

- **Not heard:** tap the prompts under the microphone, or type in the box. They reach Dhoop exactly as speech does.
- **No answer for ten seconds at the start:** speech recognition failed to start. Dhoop recovers by itself and says "Sorry, I missed that". Repeat the line.
- **The site is slow to open:** the free server was asleep. It wakes in about a minute.
- **No internet at the desk:** `npm run demo` on the laptop gives the same app through a tunnel.

## One sentence for each thing the judges score

- **Problem and fit:** 68 lakh households have applied for the rooftop solar subsidy; most cannot tell, in their own language, whether their roof and their bill make it worth it. Dhoop tells them in one conversation.
- **Innovation:** it finds the caller's own roof from space and redraws it by voice; nothing is typed.
- **Agora and Murf:** one Agora agent with voice, vision, tools and memory, restarted mid-call to change language; every word spoken by Murf Falcon in six languages.
- **It works:** deployed, and it books, quotes and remembers.
- **Scale:** every figure comes from a public source, so it works for any roof in India today.
